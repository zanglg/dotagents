# Optional read-only delegation

Use helpers only when a separable source question benefits from independent
context or avoids substantial parent reads. Otherwise investigate directly.

- Assign a bounded question with essential user constraints, source revision and
  working-state qualification, relevant evidence pointers, allowed reads and
  resource limits. State whether artifacts are actually accessible; do not fork
  the full conversation or pre-read everything merely to delegate it again.
- Helpers remain read-only: no source/report/scratch writes, experiment execution
  or recursive delegation. They return findings through the conversation/tool
  response. Follow any governing coordinator's restrictions without gaining
  additional authority from this Skill.
- Request the supporting paths/symbols, connecting reasoning, counterevidence,
  limitations and unresolved questions. Prefer concise evidence over raw source
  or logs, but do not omit material findings to meet a fixed response cap.
- The parent checks consequential claims against targeted evidence and combines
  results. Qualify observations of changing files and recheck affected claims;
  do not claim stable snapshots or independent verification the host cannot supply.

Keep the question, constraints, key evidence and next action concise under the
host's actual context limits. A recap does not clear tokens or preserve missing
files. Do not create a progress ledger or handoff automatically; explicit session
transfer is a separate operation.
