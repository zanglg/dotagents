# Read-only delegation

Delegate a bounded question when independent context or parallel investigation
offers meaningful benefit. Otherwise investigate directly.

Give the helper enough context to reason correctly: the question, essential
constraints, relevant source state, evidence access and authority limits.
Do not assume shared files or independent context the host cannot provide.

Helpers investigate and return evidence; they do not write files, execute
experiments or delegate further. A helper assignment grants no authority beyond
the governing coordinator's restrictions.

A useful return connects conclusions to traceable evidence and preserves material
counterevidence and uncertainty. Let the question determine its structure and depth.

The parent remains responsible for the answer: verify consequential claims,
reconcile conflicting or changing evidence, and disclose unresolved limits.
Only the parent may save an authorized report. Delegation does not require
additional reports, progress records or handoff files.
