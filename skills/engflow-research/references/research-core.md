# Shared source-investigation method

This reference supplies evidence standards for an active caller, not a Research
invocation or independent task. The caller keeps its authority, output ownership
and source synchronization duties. Reading it grants no report writes, experiment
execution, access/disclosure expansion or permission to change approved decisions.
It does not forbid tests separately authorized for an Implement task; those are
caller-owned verification, not read-only source investigation.

## Evidence standard

Ground claims in the target's actual implementation, applicable first-party
specifications and existing observations. General knowledge guides inspection;
it does not establish private protocol, hardware or target-specific facts.

Trace relevant code paths and governing conditions. Explain why evidence applies
to the target version/configuration and how it supports the conclusion. Seek
counterevidence; resolve contradictions or retain them explicitly. Distinguish
observations, reasoned conclusions, proposed behavior and unknowns. Static analysis
is not runtime verification.

## Bounded investigation

Inspect accessible evidence first. Investigate consequential gaps without unrelated
audits; routine diagnosis needs no special method or separate task. Stop on adequate
support or a concrete access, evidence or authority limit. Ask precise questions for
missing private facts instead of plausible guesses; pause only dependent work.

Connect the answer to reasoning and traceable source references, not a symbol list.
State limitations and the next discriminating check. Proposed runtime checks are
not executed by this read-only method; the caller must assess execution authority
and use [the experiment method](../../engflow-poc/references/experiment-core.md)
where useful.

## Integration and helpers

The caller verifies consequential claims, incorporates evidence into its authorized
plan/design/task/code work and identifies stale sources needing synchronization.
Evidence is advisory, not design approval or a grant to change requirements.
A report alone does not close invalid assumptions or prove a fix.

Optional investigation helpers are read-only, do not execute probes, write reports
or delegate further, and return evidence with uncertainty. The caller retains
responsibility; use [delegation guidance](delegation.md) if helpers materially help.
No helper or separate report is needed to apply this method.
